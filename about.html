About SeamFlow

Systems, not just advice.

SeamFlow Consulting is a business operations excellence consultancy for healthcare SMEs in Nigeria.

Our Founder

Led by Akunna Mbanefo, Founder & Principal Consultant

Connect with Akunna on LinkedIn

Akunna's path into healthcare operations started on the ground, not in a boardroom.

For about three years, she worked in HMO marketing across Nigeria's Southeast — enrolling clients, liaising directly with healthcare providers, and making sure insured patients could actually access the care they'd signed up for, not just hold a card that promised it.

She has also managed operations at an eye hospital, where she led the process of bringing HMOs in to inspect and accredit the facility — successfully onboarding three separate HMOs as approved healthcare partners under her leadership.

Alongside this role, she spent time on radio driving community preventive eye health awareness, encouraging routine checks that catch conditions like glaucoma and cataract before they become emergencies.

This combination — selling healthcare access, running a healthcare facility's day-to-day operations, and building the compliance relationships that keep a facility eligible for HMO business — provides the knowledge base to effectively run SeamFlow Consulting.

Akunna has seen, firsthand, both sides of the gap her business now closes:

What a facility needs to pass an accreditation walk-through, and what breaks down internally when no one has documented how things are supposed to run.

At SeamFlow Consulting, we design repeatable operational systems that take a facility from chaotic and owner-dependent to structured, scalable, and consistently profitable — no unnecessary complexity, no jargon. Just structure that works.

If your healthcare facility feels disorganized or overwhelming behind the scenes, it's time for a positive change.

Our Mission

Every healthcare SME in Nigeria should run like a real institution.

We believe a small privately -owned clinic deserves to display the same operational excellence as a well- structured and run teaching hospital - clearly defined processes, SOPs, measured performance, a trained workforce and a facility that patients trust enough to stay loyal to.

The Methodology

Clarity. Structure. Growth.™

The same three-stage sequence in every engagement, because a facility cannot skip a stage and expect the next one to hold.

Stage 1 — Diagnose the real gaps

A full Business Systems Audit across every department produces an honest, scored picture of where structure already exists and where it's missing — before a single SOP is written.

Stage 2 — Install the missing structure

Every department gets documented processes, SOPs and workflows aligned to the Nigerian regulatory bodies that govern them.

Stage 3 — Keep it alive

Performance measurements, a well- trained and vibrant workforce, strategies for proper brand positioning, an expansion roadmap, etc. turns the new structure into a lasting standard rather than a one-time project.
